排位規則與模擬 / Ranked rules

所有怪物穿過砲台，直接攻擊核心。砲台不會被敵人摧毀。
Enemies pass through turrets and attack only the core. Turrets cannot be destroyed.

核心 HP 與配隊 / Core HP and lineup

五種不同砲台的 HP 加總。血量強化：隨機一種的原始 HP ×10%，同時增加核心目前與最大 HP。
Sum of five distinct turret HP values. HP upgrade adds 10% of one random turret type's base HP to both current and maximum core HP.



部署規則 / Deployment rules
最多 13 座；同種類同星級融合，隨機升星，最高 10 星。20 秒一波。擊殺或撞核心消失各給 8 金幣；撞核心不派怪。
13 slots; merge matching types and stars into a random higher-star turret, up to 10 stars. Waves every 20 seconds. Kills and core collisions give 8 coins; only kills send enemies to the rival.
護盾依距離減傷，上限 24%；時間場減速上限 35%。遠程每 2.4 秒射擊核心且持續前進；Boss 光束與脈衝每 10 秒只攻擊核心。
Shield reduction is range-based, capped at 24%; slow auras cap at 35%. Ranged enemies fire at the core every 2.4 seconds while moving; boss beams and pulses target the core every 10 seconds.


49 種音效與段位 / 49 weapon voices and ranks
49 種砲台有獨立音色、節奏、材質與尾音；最多 14 聲部，依全遊戲音量設定播放。BGM 使用已裝備且擁有的樂曲，音量為主音量的 22%，交戰時暫降為 65%。
49 authored voices with distinct body, rhythm, material and tail; maximum 14 concurrent voices. Uses global audio settings and owned, equipped BGM at 22% of master volume, ducked to 65% of that during combat.
